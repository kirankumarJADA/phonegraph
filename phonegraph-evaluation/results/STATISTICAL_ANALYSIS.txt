KEY FINDING FOR DISCUSSION CHAPTER
======================================================================

PhoneGraph achieves significantly lower hallucination than all five baselines
(McNemar p<0.001 in every case), directly answering RQ1.

However, PhoneGraph's retrieval quality (P@5/MRR/nDCG@10) is statistically
indistinguishable from or marginally lower than Vanilla RAG and RAG-Fusion
(Wilcoxon: P@5 ns, MRR/nDCG@10 p<0.01 favouring the semantic-only baselines).

This indicates the hybrid graph+vector fusion does not by itself improve
ranking quality over semantic search alone. The large hallucination advantage
of PhoneGraph over Vanilla RAG (9.2% vs 42.4%), despite near-identical
retrieval, isolates the source of improvement to the constrained-generation/
prompting layer rather than the retrieval architecture. This directly
motivates the ablation study (RQ2b): isolating vector-only, graph-only,
hybrid-without-constraints, and hybrid-with-constraints conditions.
